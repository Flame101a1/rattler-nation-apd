APD Chain of Command

Understanding the department rank structure is essential for all personnel.

Rattler Nation · Austin Police Department

Purpose

All Austin Police Department personnel are expected to understand and follow the established Chain of Command. Personnel should route concerns, questions, and requests through the appropriate channels whenever possible.

Chain of Command Expectations

  • Follow lawful orders issued by supervisors.
  • Route concerns through immediate supervisors whenever practical.
  • Do not skip multiple levels of supervision without justification.
  • Higher ranking personnel maintain authority over lower ranking personnel.
  • Scene Command may temporarily override normal chain of command during incidents.
