How to Submit a Warrant

1. Choose Your Template

Click Arrest Warrant or Search Warrant above to open the type of warrant you need.

2. Make a Copy

In the top-left corner of Google Docs, select File → Make a copy. Give your copy an appropriate, descriptive name.

3. Complete the Warrant

Fill out all required details in your copy. Review the information and supporting evidence before submitting.

4. Set Sharing Permissions

Click Share in the top-right corner. Under General access, select Anyone with the link and set the role to Editor. Click Copy link, then Done.

5. Submit in Discord

Once everything is complete, post the link to your completed copy in #warrant-submission in the LEO Discord.
