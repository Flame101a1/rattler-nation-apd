How to Upload Pictures

Use PhotoToURL to turn a picture into a link that can be attached to a CID case.

  1. Open PhotoToURL.
  2. Upload the picture from your device.
  3. Copy the direct image link it gives you.
  4. Return here, choose Evidence Type as Photo, and paste the link into Evidence Link.
